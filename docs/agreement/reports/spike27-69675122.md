# Spike 27: word-stress classifier and native envelope, offline feasibility (GO / NO-GO): spike27-69675122

## Summary addendum (hand-written, 2026-11-24, after CTO accepted the NO-GO; not generated, no number changed)

- **Key finding:** on native words, the dictionary-stressed syllable is rarely the most prominent one on any single measure. On raw tuning-side natives (arm B front end, 583 words) it has the longest nucleus 49.9% of the time, the highest f0 residual 52.3%, the loudest nucleus 64.2% and the flattest spectrum 70.2%. A plain position baseline ("stress the first syllable") is right **80.8%** of the time. These measures carry less stress information than word position does, which is why no arm separates natural from misplaced stress.
- **The identity-PSOLA artefact check (AUC 0.500, "MET" below) was vacuous.** Identity PSOLA reproduces its input exactly, so processed and raw audio are the same and the AUC is 0.5 by construction. **The real artefact check is the swap-and-back round trip** (swap two nuclei, then swap them back, against raw): **arm B 0.468** (no learnable artefact) and **arm A 0.604** (above the 0.6 line, so arm A partly learned PSOLA artefacts rather than stress).
- **speechocean762 is the source of the 134 stress-wrong words.** Its correctly stressed set is a **same-corpus control, not an independent test set**: same speakers' population, recording setup and annotation scheme. That's also why its misplaced set is empty once the 134's utterances are dropped (gate 2, speechocean762 misplaced: NOT EVALUATED).
